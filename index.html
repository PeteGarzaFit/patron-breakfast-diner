The menu

Something for every kind of morning.

  • Two-Egg Breakfast

    Eggs any style with bacon or sausage, crispy hash browns and toast.

  • Fluffy Pancakes

    Short or tall stack, whipped butter and warm maple syrup.

  • Build-Your-Own Omelet

    Three eggs folded with your choice of fillings, served with hash browns and toast.

  • Biscuits & Gravy

    Buttermilk biscuits smothered in peppery country sausage gravy.

  • French Toast

    Thick-cut, golden and dusted with powdered sugar.

  • Hash Brown Skillet

    Crispy hash browns, eggs, cheese and your choice of meat.

  • Menudo House favorite

    Slow-simmered beef tripe and hominy in a rich red chile broth, with onion, cilantro, oregano, lime and warm tortillas.

  • Chilaquiles

    Crisp tortilla chips tossed in salsa roja or verde, topped with eggs, queso fresco and crema.

  • Huevos Rancheros

    Fried eggs over corn tortillas with ranchero sauce, beans and rice.

  • Breakfast Tacos

    Warm flour tortillas with egg and your pick: potato, bacon, chorizo, or bean and cheese.

  • Migas

    Eggs scrambled with tortilla strips, peppers, onion and melted cheese.

  • Charro Beans & Rice

    Slow-cooked pinto beans with savory bits, served with a dome of white rice.

  • Nachos Grande Famous

    A mountain of chips with lettuce, pico de gallo, jalapeños, crema and guacamole.

  • Classic Cheeseburger

    Griddled beef patty, American cheese, lettuce, tomato and red onion, with crinkle-cut fries.

  • Crispy Chicken Salad

    Crisp greens, tomato, cheese and golden fried chicken with your choice of dressing.

  • Quesadilla

    A grilled flour tortilla packed with melted cheese and your choice of filling.

  • Homemade Desserts

    Baked in-house. Ask your server what's fresh today.

  • Fresh-Brewed Coffee

    Hot, strong and bottomless.

  • Café de Olla

    Spiced with cinnamon and piloncillo.

  • Horchata

    Sweet, creamy and cinnamon-kissed.

  • Orange Juice

    Bright, cold and the perfect start.

Our diner

Like stepping into the diners we remember.

Walk into Patron Breakfast Diner and you'll feel like you've gone back in time. There's warm wood on the walls, a coffee cup that's never empty, and a table set for friends and family.

We opened in 2024 with one simple promise: start your day right. That means real food made fresh, whether you're craving a stack of pancakes, a bowl of menudo, or a burger before the afternoon rush.
